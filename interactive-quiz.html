The Great Drone Race

Last month, our city hosted the annual drone racing championship. It was an absolutely thrilling experience for everyone! Our team worked carefully on our drone, "Aero," for weeks. We thought Aero was extremely fast, but when we saw the other competitors, we were quite anxious. The defending champion's drone, "Goliath," was enormous and looked very intimidating. (1) Goliath was much bulkier and heavier than Aero. However, Goliath was nowhere near as nimble as Aero during the first obstacle ring.

Another drone, named "Vortex," zipped perfectly through the tricky hoops. (2) Vortex finished the track in far less time than Goliath. Honestly, Vortex was just as swift as a falcon. Our drone, Aero, was fairly good in this round, but its turns were slightly wider. It was not quite as precise as Vortex when navigating the narrow tunnels.

During the final lap, the audience was silent; we could hardly blink. Aero and Vortex were neck and neck the whole way. At the finish line, they were nearly tied! (3) Aero's top speed was slightly lower than Vortex's top speed. But in the end, Vortex won the gold medal. Interestingly, their design was not as flashy as ours, but it was undeniably effective.

We learned a lot from this event. (4) Our next drone will definitely be much more aerodynamic than our current one. We are already planning the new frame. It is going to be not nearly as heavy as Goliath, but it will be just as resilient!
